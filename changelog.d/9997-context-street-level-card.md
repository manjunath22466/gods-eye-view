- A street-level photo from any provider gets its own Context card. With
  Context open, a click on a coverage line or a cone, OPEN or voice brings up
  the card once the photo has loaded, unless something else was picked
  meanwhile; a collapsed Context stays closed. The card's top image is the
  photo viewer itself, its source line names the provider and capture date,
  and More details adds heading and whether the photo is flat or 360°. Steps
  through the photo move its card along without reopening Context or changing
  its tab. The card's × closes the photo. Picking something else or using
  Escape on the map returns the open photo to the Street Level panel
  (manjunath22466, #9997).
- Street Level now appears in Context's count and Nearby: the last 12 photos
  opened this session, then one row per coverage line near the view. A card
  picked from Nearby says "Loading photo..." while its photo opens, and why
  when none opens, with Retry (manjunath22466, #9997).
- A place card shows a KartaView or Panoramax photo opened this session within
  150 m on top, otherwise a close-up map. Every still of a street photo
  carries its full credit: the provider's credit as the globe shows it, then
  who took it and its licence when known (manjunath22466, #9997).
