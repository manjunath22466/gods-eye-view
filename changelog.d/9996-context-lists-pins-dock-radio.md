- Context's tabs are one line: Inspect at the start, then Camera settings
  for an open camera card (its projection, coverage, auto-hop, calibration
  with a label and its status in words, and the scene summary as short
  labelled lines), a list layer's tab and Pinned, the row's end fading
  while more are out of view. While Space Missions, Weather, News, Recent
  Imagery or Cameras is on, its list is a tab, and with two or more of
  those on, one Lists tab holds a sub-tab per layer (Space Missions · 12 holds
  the mission roster and a selected mission's card); every list tab names
  its count or why it has none. A list layer's
  tab takes Context over only when a person turns that layer on; one a link
  or a restore brings back adds its tab quietly, and Cameras never take it
  over. Pin sends cameras, stations, places and other picks to
  a Pinned tab (no limit; pins never play or poll; unpinning offers Undo, and
  Clear all has Undo too), and a camera feed, cockpit view or street-level
  photo can be docked bottom right while other things are inspected; a
  docked camera stays live with Cameras off while other pins pause.
  Replacing a docked street-level photo may briefly show a loading state
  in the dock, while the card and share link keep the original until the
  new photo appears; Cancel, a failure or a timeout (one shared 10 s
  deadline for quick successive picks) restores the original photo,
  and Cancel is hidden while the original is being restored. If the
  original cannot be restored either, the dock keeps its card and share
  link and shows the photo's saved still when there is one, or an "Image
  could not be opened" error. A replacement that does not load says so on
  the dock. Feed state is
  one dot everywhere (live green and pulsing, paused amber, no feed red), and
  a paused picture has a one-line Paused chip. The dock and the radio mini
  player sit at the foot of the right column under an open Context, which
  shrinks to make room in one step. Radio's stations show in Context,
  nearest first, with a mini player from the moment a station is selected or
  heard, a volume control and Radio's source (Internet stations or Local
  receiver) on its Data Layers row; a station pinned while it plays keeps
  playing through Radio off and other picks until the mini player's Close.
  Esc clears the pick before the search while Inspect shows it, and leaves
  it on the other tabs; a calibration value being typed, an open volume
  slider and a Recent Imagery preview or box keep their own Esc. Folded, Context's name opens it; its scrollbar no longer
  flashes as values update. Space Missions stays an ordinary layer: turning
  it on or off leaves the user's other layers as they are, except
  Satellites, which it switches on for its orbit data (no dots or orbits
  drawn) and restores when it turns off. The Weather list opens with a short
  briefing, the Recent Imagery list offers to draw an area, the News list
  folds its filters into one row and gains Prev and Next on its Data Layers
  row, a selected mission is a standard card (the launch site, Replay · Fly
  to · Pin, its launch time, orbit and status), and the mapped installations'
  nearby search is also a chip on their Data Layers row. Also changed: the
  dock sits above the corner row, Cockpit hides the corner row and the
  Stories entry, and its keyboard hint clears the map credits; on a phone the
  logo clears the radio wave (manjunath22466, #9996).
